/ HOW DOES IT WORK?

THE DOWNWARD MOVEMENT OF THE LEVER IS ENABLED BY AN INTERNAL MECHANICAL SYSTEM.

THE ENTIRE OUTER SHELL IS FIXED TO A HIDDEN “LEVER SUPPORT” THAT SLIDES ALONG A GUIDE, DRIVEN BY THE “MAIN MOVING PART“ CONNECTED TO BOTH THE CHASSIS AND THE SUPPORT.

WHEN THE LEVER IS FULLY LOWERED, A “LOCKING LATCH SYSTEM” HOLDS IT IN PLACE DURING TOASTING, RELEASING IT AT THE END OF THE CYCLE TO PRODUCE THE ICONIC
POP-UP MOTION.

/ WHAT ABOUT THE CRUMBS?

CRUMBS ARE COLLECTED IN A DEDICATED TRAY LOCATED BEHIND THE TOASTER.

THE TRAY CAN BE EASILY ACCESSED AND EMPTIED AT ANY TIME: A SIMPLE PRESS RELEASES IT PARTIALLY FROM THE MAIN BODY, MAKING IT VISIBLE AND EASY FOR THE USER TO GRASP AND REMOVE.

/ HOW DOES IT WORK?
